## Completion gate

1. Audit every applicable normative v1 clause, including adopted later directions,
   into a precise contract, supported-profile dependency or justified exclusion.
   Historical prose and deferred feature designs are not automatically v1 work.
2. Resolve material behavior choices with the user. No recommendation, absent
   reply, abstract witness or tool result silently approves a new policy.
3. Complete the operation/type/event and outcome/projection relations, including
   malformed, stale, denied, duplicate, late, overload, cancellation, unknown,
   fault and unsupported cases at each applicable boundary.
4. Define supported host/provider/platform/Unicode/resource profiles or explicit
   profile parameters and their validity rules. An unspecified number cannot be
   presented as a measured bound; an unspecified guarantee cannot be assumed true.
5. Link preconditions, invariants, progress assumptions, security/privacy risks,
   observation points and independently derived conformance/refinement evidence.
6. Reconcile requirements, architecture, specification, model scope and current
   hashes, then perform the adversarial review. Existing abstract proof success
   does not establish all new contract clauses or implementation readiness.
