# Design-review ledger — historical

The [original ledger](specification-closure-ledger.history.md) is preserved for
its design decisions and review provenance. Its staged/open statements and
percentage estimates are historical, not the current implementation work queue.

Use the [README](../README.md) for current packages, runnable paths and limitations,
the [testing guide](engine-test-plan.md) for native acceptance, and actual Rust
code/tests for implemented behavior. The provisional native engine's seven
delivery gates closed; that does not establish the broader editor release.

The [wider release obligations](../v1-release-gates.md) and adopted requirements
still apply. Prototype frontends do not waive their remaining capability and
platform qualification. Release-profile choices and actual GUI qualification must
remain explicit; historical evidence does not close them.

The root specification audit, coverage, residuals, decisions and release-gate
files are retained as frozen design-review inputs. Do not interpret their old
implementation status as current or rewrite their hashes for routine progress.
Keep new implementation history in code/tests rather than another running ledger.
