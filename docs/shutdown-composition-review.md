## Scope and captured decisions

This composition target shuts down one explicitly identified local authority and
engine incarnation. Its affected scope is all that authority's live and retained
owners, together with its admission domain for future ordinary work/owner creation.
Capture the complete bounded actual inventory at acceptance, including document
incarnations, relevant lifecycle/source/serialization/annotation/binding versions
and work/retention obligations. The fence must also reject new creation, attachment,
membership or activation that would enter or add ordinary work to this authority;
it is not just a flag on the initially enumerated documents. Protected evidence
and discharge are the explicit exceptions below.

A workspace name, current focus or pathname is not this control scope. Narrower
document/view/workspace closure retains the existing F17 lifecycle contracts;
this reference does not silently add membership-following F18 container scopes.
Any separately advertised narrower shutdown must supply its own exact scope and
entry/final-admission fence. Closing one view/workspace does not imply authority
shutdown or consent to discard its shared document. Other authorities remain
outside this shutdown fence.

Each affected document has separate source and annotation decisions: save,
explicitly authorized discard, or cancel shutdown before disposition acceptance.
Save references the ordinary captured-save contracts and their actual evidence;
it does not trigger an implicit autosave. Before accepting/fencing a save choice,
require either existing qualified savedness for the approved intended output or
an exact matching ordinary save already admitted under its full reservation and
original capture. If absent, remain before acceptance and report the ordinary
save action needed for the approved source/annotation versions. The host can submit
those explicitly authorized ordinary saves, then retry shutdown acceptance.
Do not invent a post-fence fresh-save exception. If shutdown acceptance later
refuses, those independently admitted saves retain their actual ordinary outcomes;
the refusal cannot imply that they were never admitted or undo their effects.

An incomplete decision set produces a
structured needs-decision result without destructive disposal or an admission
fence. Unrelated work can continue during this decision phase.

Bind consent to exact acknowledged versions, not a movable latest document. At
disposition acceptance and again before destructive disposal, check the relevant
current identities/versions and current authority. An intervening authored or
relevant binding/configuration change requires a fresh decision; equal bytes do
not repair stale consent. Save readiness uses the existing qualified baseline/
exact-output-equality rules, not a new requirement for a same-revision disk token.
Saved source alone cannot satisfy annotation disposition.

## Admission fence and transition relation

The names below describe internal semantic stages, not selected public status
strings. Each control call validates a closed input and performs bounded work.

| Stage or event | Guard and transition | Facts that remain unchanged |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inspect | Validate control authority, complete bounded scope and supported wait/retention profile. Produce needs-decision or a complete captured disposition candidate. | No discard, effect launch or claim of shutdown acceptance. |
| Cancel before acceptance | Validate the original pending control identity; end that decision operation. | Existing documents, work and grants remain governed by their normal contracts. |
| Accept disposition | At one serialized local authority boundary, revalidate scope/consent/current control and each save-choice prerequisite above; reserve the complete control/report/retention obligation and establish the authority admission fence, including future ordinary entry. Failure before fence installation leaves the prior admission state intact. | No source/annotation mutation, effect acknowledgment, worker discharge or presumed persistence; independently admitted ordinary saves are not rolled back. |
| Drain | Refuse new ordinary work into the fenced scope. Cancel only eligible replaceable queued work through its actual owner; service required evidence, protected cleanup and already admitted eligible effects under their existing contracts. | Captures, original conditions, issued origins and historical outcomes cannot be rewritten. |
| Apply a document disposition | Recheck exact consent, independent source/annotation readiness and the complete actual retention/disposal guards. Apply the existing F17 disposal relation or retain that owner with its explicit unresolved disposition. | Peer documents, previously applied outcomes and retained provenance/effect evidence are not rolled back. |
| Wait cutoff or unavailable clock | End only waiting. Return a bounded current-authorized report at the cut and retain the original admission fence and unresolved owners/charges. Invalid clock evidence is not elapsed time or an unlimited extension. | Unknown effects, worker holds, dirty-state obligations and delivery/retention duties remain owned. |
| Complete or explicitly retained | Complete only the requested closure whose disposition and lifetime obligations are satisfied. Otherwise report the outstanding/unknown/retained disposition without asserting complete shutdown. | No process termination, crash recovery, secure erasure or zero-allocation claim. |
| Forced-close request | Require explicit current host authority and a supported retention/transfer contract for every continuing obligation before releasing its old owner. Without that contract, refuse the requested release and retain responsibility. | Local discard cannot retract an issued write, create a new owner or manufacture a transfer witness. |

The fence applies to new admission and the relevant final publication/dispatch
checks, not just the ingress queue. A previously prepared authored edit cannot
publish into a fenced target through a stale positive flag. A multi-object edit
touching a fenced participant cannot partly publish elsewhere. Work in other
authorities continues under its normal dependencies and shared resource bounds.

The fence does not cancel every admitted save. An eligible already admitted
captured save may still need dispatch/drain to fulfill the explicit save choice;
it retains its payload, current launch checks and original conditions. No new
ordinary work or fresh payload is smuggled into the scope as shutdown cleanup.
Allow its ordinary authenticated evidence intake, actual qualified baseline
installation and required retention/claim bookkeeping after the fence. These
transitions preserve authored source/annotations and still require the exact
current binding, capture, authority and native baseline guards; they cannot
resurrect a disposed owner or turn an unrelated SaveAs/rebind into cleanup.
Protected evidence, cancellation, observation stop and discharge remain callable
without spare ordinary capacity or permission to disclose document contents.

Dispositions across documents are not a new atomic multi-file persistence claim.
If individual disposals progress before another owner blocks, preserve their actual
results and report the remaining owners. Do not describe the whole operation as
unapplied or roll back already applied dispositions. A cancelled wait likewise
does not reopen admission or resurrect a disposed incarnation. Any separately
supported resumption needs its own checked contract; it is not implicit rollback.

## Retained ownership and bounded waiting

Each continuing obligation retains its original actual owner unless an existing
supported transfer explicitly establishes the successor, retained evidence,
authority and discharge of the predecessor. A result labelled retained is not a
license for the embedding to free the engine, callback state or provider assets.
If continued protected service needs that owner, the host must keep it alive.
V1 does not invent a durable ledger, supervisor service or process-wide migration.

Distinguish queued-never-issued, issued unresolved effect, settled result,
separately attested quiescence, retained content/history, and undrained delivery.
Cancellation can end an eligible queued operation without proving issued work
stopped. Effect resolution, quiescence and delivery/retention discharge each use
their own witnesses. Unknown/contradictory effects cannot become safe to retry,
release a destination key or retire merely because shutdown reaches its cutoff.
Late authentic evidence keeps its original attribution after waiting ends.

One non-confusable shutdown identity binds the authority incarnation, captured
intent/phase, original fence decision and wait profile. Wait time starts at
successful disposition/fence acceptance, not at each drain, report or retry call.
Retain the original clock epoch/start and nonrenewable remaining budget. Identical
acceptance retries reuse the same accepted control; changed intent cannot silently
replace it. Repeated calls cannot reinstall the fence, reserve twice, reapply a
document disposal or reset elapsed time. An explicit forced-close decision is a
separately validated action on that existing control, not a renewed wait budget.
Retain spent identity and per-document progress through cutoff until its actual
control/evidence obligations permit protected retirement.

The wait profile supplies finite capture/work/report limits and, where a timed
guarantee is requested, a valid local monotonic clock/epoch and suspend contract.
Do not admit an unavailable stronger timing guarantee as an unlimited wait.
Each step processes only admitted finite inventories; live progress additionally
requires actual protected host service and finite/cooperative execution. The
engine does not prove native callback preemption or that an arbitrary worker
eventually returns. No numerical timeout or SLO is selected here.

Retain the cutoff observation as an immutable historical cut. Later authentic
evidence can change current knowledge, not rewrite what that cutoff reported.
Any later current-status query must identify its separate cut and pass current
release; retries cannot silently present refreshed data as the original report.

The preservation argument is conditional on complete actual inventories, truthful
policy/identity/clock/discharge suppliers and serialized engine boundaries.
Acceptance establishes a fence before reporting acceptance. Every later supported
admission consults that fence, and disposal revalidates exact consent, so a stale
edit or stale discard decision cannot cross either boundary. Ordinary owner
relations preserve historical evidence and release only justified obligations.
The cutoff transition frames those relations, so ending waiting cannot invent a
refund, acknowledgment or quiescence fact. Per-call finite work is distinct from
eventual worker termination. The full composition remains open. The selected
executable reference below covers source writers and retained disposal, not
every owner class.

The required attachment relation has these consequences:

1. Successful native factory acquisition and first attachment establish a bounded,
   actual-domain edge at the same serialized boundary as admission. Rejected
   acquisition leaves neither a visible owner nor an unexplained reservation.
   Future acquisition and lazy ordinary pool creation consult the same absorbing
   fence. A caller-supplied list or Boolean cannot establish completeness.
2. Each edge preserves the actual owner identity, domain incarnation and original
   responsibility. Shared writers and reader/provenance relations permit several
   distinct domain edges. Removing one edge does not discharge the shared node.
   Search leases, baseline producer pins, task bases and observer contexts remain
   explicit dependencies on their original issuers.
3. Inventory frames and capture/report work require declared aggregate funding.
   Reuse actual parent holds where they exist; separately bounded local ledgers
   still need aggregate attachment accounting. Do not repurpose invocation slot
   or lifetime issuance limits as owner-count limits or select numeric defaults.
4. Acceptance captures all current edges and excludes future ordinary entry at
   one boundary. Every eventual release still uses the native discharge relation.
   An owner without whole-owner teardown remains explicitly retained; deleting a
   map entry, resetting issuance or returning an empty task list is not discharge.
5. Protected service operates on the already owned obligations and their admitted
   reservations. It cannot create an unrelated ordinary owner or a new save.

### Candidate shared writer funding relation

Adopted 0.218 uses one immutable sponsoring actual domain for each actual
shared writer at its selected reference scope.
Requirements §7.5, specification §17.2 and the resource-profile accounting laws
require aggregate funding and truthful shared retention; they do not uniquely
choose the payer. A read-only challenge confirmed that distinction.

The writer node and its domain attachments are separate records. Before native
use, the sponsor reserves one complete declared writer envelope, including its
codec, bounded tasks/claims, spent origins and shared-node metadata. Each domain
reserves its own attachment/reference metadata. Additional independently applicable
scope-demand charges remain required; naming the writer neither duplicates its
whole-node reservation nor creates quota. A local writer ceiling alone is not
evidence of a parent reservation. All arithmetic must be finite and checked, and
the complete envelope must be derived before admission rather than inferred from
current empty usage.

The sponsor is fixed at the first successful native binding. Reserve funding and
the original domain edge before exposing that binding; failure unwinds only new
uncommitted reservations and leaves existing writer state unchanged. Existing
writers cannot be silently adopted after unfunded native use. Other-domain binding
requires the original actual writer and actual domain records, complete bounds
and the ordinary admission fence, not equal authority strings or copied markers.
Simultaneous first attachments serialize so they cannot establish two sponsors or
two whole-writer charges.

Removing an attachment does not release shared funding or peer tasks, codecs,
provenance, spent origins or destination barriers. The sponsor and its reservation
remain retained through its own shutdown while the writer still requires them.
There is no whole-writer retirement or sponsor-transfer contract in the existing
reference; therefore this refinement cannot report either operation as complete
or refund the whole-node reservation. Protected task/evidence discharge retains
its existing guards and does not reopen ordinary admission.

This deliberately trades capacity and sponsor lifetime for a simple stable
accounting owner. Charging the whole writer to every domain would penalize aliases;
a separate authority-wide funding owner could avoid the sponsor dependency but
would require an additional owner, capacity and lifetime contract. Neither
alternative is necessary for this candidate integration. The sponsor must be
selected by trusted composition, not a caller-controlled authority label.

The editable reference now contains actual node/edge records and a conservative
reservation for the native writer/codec ledgers, bounded spent-origin/destination
keys and node/edge metadata. This is not total transient or physical memory
accounting. It checks first binding and reentry, parent capacity and overflow,
actual peer tasks surviving sponsor fencing, and eligible source-save drain and
baseline adoption after the fence. Native source-reader construction and native
destination preparation establish binding; direct ordinary annotation admission
requires an existing binding. Provenance staging validates before sponsor funding.
The standalone supplied-root annotation-export helper remains legacy composition;
native export enters through the bound native destination path. Existing save
regressions and mutation banks are reused. Read-only challenge found and verified
the annotation-ingress and provenance-staging corrections, then found no further
must-fix within that scope. This selected relation is now qualified and adopted
in 0.218; complete shutdown and whole-writer retirement remain open.

The editable successor also attaches native publication observers through private
wrappers. It derives roots from the actual domain, binds original caller scope
before consulting current loss facts, and retains funding after stop or outcome
retirement. Its local outcome summary resolves an actual terminal invocation
ticket rather than accepting caller-supplied facts. It refuses unsupported rich
producer facets; current recipient authorization remains a supplier premise.
This does not complete heterogeneous F19 dispatch or cross-family outcome assembly.

Remaining acceptance requires composition with all supported existing-owner
ordinary admission/final guards, dependency
closure and the complete shutdown controller. No complete shutdown follows from
the shared-writer checks. This design does not add a public verb, numeric default,
automatic transfer, whole-writer refund or production memory-bound claim.
