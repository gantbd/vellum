# Retained annotation and combined projection evidence

Existing F02 retained selection resolves an original available annotation/combined
context in the same actual owner, domain and pool. The retained permitted
projection supplies the new copy; newer root content and caller-supplied revisions
cannot refresh it or reconstruct missing fields. No public verb, transport,
frontend mode or numeric default is added.

## Authority and class narrowing

The request's data classes must be a nonempty unique subset of the original's
admitted classes. Recognized request/selection and exact original scope precede
accepted loss. Current whole-projection eligibility covers all classes/facets of
the original, even when acquiring a narrower copy. Matching labels are binding,
not grants; host identity/policy truth and complete notification remain premises.

Accepted bound loss closes the original before checking new admission capacity.
Unknown facts and transient resource refusal withhold without establishing loss.
New preparation reservation remains independently exact true. Actual owner fault
or disposal fences reacquisition and delivery under stale positive facts.

Narrowing removes excluded raw values and their private query projection. A
source-only copy holds no annotations, needs no subsequent annotation grant, and
rejects F03 before observing out-of-class loss. Annotation-only copies similarly
retain no source or later source-grant dependency. Reacquiring a narrowed copy
cannot restore removed classes/facets. The new copy still requires its own current
scope/interpretation, admitted class authority and actual document liveness.

## Installation and lifetime

Bound request/facts, selected values, scope, query projection and the entire reply.
Stage complete context/selector registry candidates before assignment; a staging
surprise fences the owner without a partial live registry. Fresh record/continuation
maps bind only the new context; original selectors cannot cross it. Successful
acquisition spends one context/selector/issuance unit in the already reserved pool.
Ordinary refusal installs nothing and spends no issuance.

The new owned copy does not borrow the original's stop state or add a hold on it.
Original retirement cannot erase values owned by the copy. Internal search holds,
context retirement and pool discharge remain distinct protected obligations.
Never refund spent issuance or another owner's reservation. Existing logical
envelopes are not measured heap size or complete global/host accounting.

F02/F05 consume the exact retained source cut after newer publication and original
retirement. F11/F12 validate native managed ownership/currentness before preparation
and at service. Read retention does not authorize writes, repair stale companions
or grant an independent prepared-consumer lease.

## Instrumentation and remaining gates

IF-02/03/05 and PATH-02/07 separate retained acquisition, content handoff, search
evaluation and dependent editing. INS-01/03/05/06/08/13 distinguish admission,
release, evaluation, current use, retention and protected discharge. MELT/golden/
RED/USE may record finite operation/disposition, latency, logical saturation,
issuance exhaustion and held obligations; bodies, source, selectors and identities
are not metric labels. Diagnostic evidence supplies no authority. Actual telemetry,
clocks, overhead and measured ceilings remain external gates.

The substrate probe excludes import/setup and arbitrary host APIs; its selected
staging behavior is not general fault containment. Arbitrary historical publication
lookup, coherent version-set ownership, asynchronous protocols and complete public
codecs/results/events remain required. Actual host/policy/identity/notification,
physical/global accounting, production concurrency/crash behavior and independent
assurance remain gates. No whole residual family is closed by this evidence.
