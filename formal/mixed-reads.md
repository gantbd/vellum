## Finite lattice scope

For fixed valid instances U and current authorized subset C, selection sets form
the bounded Boolean lattice P(U), and successful sets are P(C). Intersection is
meet, union is join, bottom is empty and the successful top is C. Relative
complement is taken within the applicable top. The checker exercises this finite
selection rule, not a whole-engine closure or termination theorem. Authority loss,
other gates, effects and semantic discovery remain separate contracts; callers
cannot remove a dependency from an already captured intent to evade refusal.

## Instrumentation and remaining gates

INS-01/02/03/08/09/12/13 distinguish advice, admission, current validation, changed/
unchanged publication, refusal and retained work. MELT/golden/RED/USE preserve
query/publication populations and bounded resource measurements. STRIDE/LINDDUN
and STPA/FMEA include omitted/substituted predicates, version replay, forged
observation grants, premature success and read-only participant corruption.
Ordinary labels contain bounded codes, not identities, versions, source or grants.

Automatic complete footprint discovery, other provider/semantic domains, actual
host/policy/membership truth, complete public products, physical/concurrent/crash
refinement and independent Cleanroom assurance remain gates. No solver condition
is added; the existing 295 conditions/fourteen manifests retain their scopes.
