## Current roots and independent relationships

Managed lifecycle roots require explicit document identity and Boolean liveness,
unique document identities, bounded document/relation/root/request/source/tag
inventories and safe publication counters. Requests have exact closed per-operation
fields. Current authority/scope/continuity/stop, resources/companions/versions/reply,
lifecycle permission and complete relevant inventory coverage gate each transition.
Actual policy/identity/coverage and serialized current-root installation remain
host premises. A field asserting completeness is not proof that an adapter supplied
every obligation or routed every writer through the shared coordinator.

Scratch creation validates logical scalar UTF-8 through the actual source-codec
state table, but does not interpret file signatures or require successful future
serialization. A literal leading U+FEFF with absent-signature settings remains
valid authored source, even though that output tuple later refuses under the
adopted codec rule. Preserve prose, mixed languages, fences, line endings and final
newline absence without normalization or semantic ownership splitting. Use explicit
profile/BOM/generated-newline settings, fresh allocation and source/settings/annotation
generation 1. Check live and retired identities, capacity and safe allocator advance.
Create an unbound owner with empty annotations and no persistence baseline; do not
implicitly add a view/workspace, perform I/O or create edit history.

Views are already registered under the authority. Attachment validates current
source and view association generations, independent read/write authority and the
actual selected positional normalizer. Never silently take a view from another
document. Attachment and detachment advance association, selection and viewport
generations; this prevents a detached/re-attached same-view/same-document ABA from
reviving an old selection or scroll plan. Detachment leaves a registered unassociated
view, not a new document or disposed owner. Native focus/IME/geometry/input-context
companions still need their actual host contract behind companion readiness.

Workspace membership uses the actual current workspace identity/generation and
member set with independent membership and workspace-read permission. An already
satisfied intent is unchanged. Add/remove only the member relation and workspace
generation; preserve source, annotations, bindings and other workspaces. No global
document lifecycle generation is advanced for independent view/workspace relation
changes. Closing the last view or removing the last membership does not dispose
an admitted owner. The separation of loaded buffers from visible windows has
direct [Neovim precedent](https://neovim.io/doc/user/windows/); the stronger agent/
obligation retention contract remains Vellum's adopted design, not a copied native
buffer-deletion guarantee.

## Retained disposal and external-effect barriers

Disposal validates exact source, annotation-set, serialization, binding and current
lifecycle generations. Source and annotation dispositions are separate retain or
explicitly authorized discard intents. Saved source never substitutes for annotation
permission. Both intents in this reference stop live mutation while retaining
historical bytes; discard authority is not a promise of immediate or secure erasure.
Require complete supported retention for effects, history, snapshots and providers,
current permission to detach all affected views/memberships, observer fencing and
complete binding coverage. Missing support refuses the entire current-root change.

Publish live=false, one fresh lifecycle generation, a retained-disposed phase,
historical binding evidence and a non-reuse identity tombstone. Detach current
views with fresh generations and remove workspace memberships with fresh workspace
generations. Preserve source/settings/annotations/history/frontiers/baseline,
unrelated documents and actual peer work/evidence owners. Retained data stays in
the same bounded authority root and peer owners; there is no automatic migration
to a durable ledger, new ownership authority, inferred write completion or worker
cancellation. Root capacity counts retained tombstones and can backpressure.

The old destination key remains reserved after disposal. Actual read-owner lookup
does not treat its disposed binding owner as known absence. Binding release checks
current expected binding, exact unique registry row, current coverage/permissions
and the actual private source-save coordinator. Every admitted same-key write must
be a stopped never-dispatched task or have known application plus attested no-future-
effect disposition and uncontested destination knowledge. Unknowns, conflicts or
nonstopped queued captures refuse. Cancellation and quiescence alone do not permit
release. Conversely safely resolved effects can release a key while their workers
remain unquiet; execution slots and retained charges stay held independently.

Remove only that exact owner row and clear the current binding; retain the old
binding in lifecycle provenance. This is not a disk rename, rebind, SaveAs or save.
An old read activation cannot resurrect its disposed incarnation after key release.
A fresh admitted object uses a non-confusable allocation. Full replacement/rebind/
SaveAs source/destination preparation remains a subsequent specification product.

## Provenance holds and concrete mutation fencing

Read and write retirement now recognize the retained-disposed root as a provenance
holder, using its retained binding even after the destination key is released.
The actual producing read and acknowledged-write tickets remain pinned; removing
a live binding is not permission to erase required historical evidence. Explicit
provenance release requires no current binding and independent history/snapshot/
read/write/provider evidence discharge. Only then does the root stop pinning those
peer records. Their own quiescence, delivery-drain, retained-dependency and authority
checks still apply before retirement. No memory reclamation, secure erasure,
successful persistence or consumer-contract truth follows from that local release.

Actual composed source/annotation publication and history reversal now refuse an
explicitly disposed document. Current selection/viewport, analysis/geometry and
new serialization gates likewise check the concrete marker. A falsely positive
supplied liveness flag cannot override it. Earlier small standalone fixtures omit
the marker and retain their explicit supplied-liveness premises; this is not a
proof of public hostile decoding, all adapters or every native companion. Managed
lifecycle roots themselves require explicit liveness and cannot omit that field.
Pure retained artifacts and attributable late effect receipts keep their separate
historical scope: they cannot publish into the disposed/new incarnation, but caller
or document death does not erase admitted work or trustworthy late evidence.

## Conditional arguments and executable evidence

Under valid complete current roots and trusted identity/policy/coverage, retention
contracts, provider safe-disposition facts and serialized installation:

1. Fresh allocation checks all actual live/retired identities and checked counters,
   so induction over creation/disposal prevents identity reuse/resurrection. No
   visibility or membership transition changes document identity or live mutation.
2. Current scoped relation updates change only their selected relation/generation
   and publication. Selection/viewport generations advance across association ABA;
   an old owned plan cannot reapply after detach/reattach to the same identities.
3. Disposal changes admission/lifecycle/relations in one copied root and preserves
   authored/history/effect data. Concrete disposed markers fence current managed
   publishers even when supplied gates are positive. Late receipts retain old
   attribution, not new mutation authority or persistence-baseline applicability.
4. The actual source-save task predicate prevents release of an unresolved same-key
   effect. It uses the complete-coordinator premise, not a workspace queue or a
   claim that quiescence established nonapplication. Key release preserves read/
   write provenance holds until their explicit independent discharge.
5. Finite scans, UTF-8 states, normalizer limits, retained-root/relation sizes and
   checked counters bound each local transition. External completion, real policy,
   native halting, physical accounting and arbitrary lattice/categorical closure
   are not consequences of this finite vocabulary.

These are authored conditional arguments, not machine-checked unbounded induction.
