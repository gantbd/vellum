## Release and recovery

At handoff recheck current context/epoch, authority, continuity, liveness, retention,
transfer and recipient release. Outcome permissions meet the capture ceiling;
broadening cannot enlarge an old report. Observed context/authorization invalidity
at event/outcome release or root observation permanently fences the observer and
clears its queue. Regrant needs a new observer and baseline. Owner stop requires
no content grant and never stops producer work.

Saturation, unrepresentable projection/frame or issuance exhaustion creates an
explicit gap and clears queued frames. Resync requires current baseline authority/
capacity, replaces the baseline and clears all older queued frames before resuming.
It cannot overcome exhausted issuance. Gap is a control result, not a missing-event
count or raw global cursor. Resync does not refresh/revive an old queued payload.
Transient resource unavailability at root observation marks a gap without permanently
revoking the observer; release capacity refusal retains an existing queued frame.
