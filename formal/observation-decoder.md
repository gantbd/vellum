Existing F06 typed/dynamic decoding uses the actual shared observation reservation.

without competing for ordinary spare capacity. This uses the same native observer
and producer ledger, not another execution mode, per-file engine or transport.

owner/capacity-activation authority. Native-only bootstrap preserves its earlier

Fixture sizes do not select numeric product defaults.

producer charge separately from workspace. Known native refusal refunds reusable
occupancy, not consumption. Unknown setup retains the entire reservation and

## Execution and protected retirement

The exact reservation binds immutable limits to its native context. Decode using
already paid workspace without a new ordinary admission. Current native policy,
transfer/retention and control permission still apply. Minimal control remains
available with ordinary readiness/resources absent, zero spare bytes/work/fanout
and stopped new admission. Another quarantined setup cannot disable an already
active protected route.

Hold busy state through bounded normalization, actual-owner authority comparison,
copied policy/facts and fixed native read/wait-start. Preserve opaque roles instead
of replacing foreign request context from the reservation. Foreign/malformed
frames cannot supply loss observations to a different valid context. Known native
revocation remains absorbing. Same-reservation reentry and parent retirement while
busy are unavailable.

Unexpected pre-dispatch failure records a decoder fault, fences the actual
observer and ends wait eligibility. Clear busy on every exit, retaining paid
native/decoder capacity, spent issuance and ended waiters. Protected waiter drain
and actual native retirement precede whole shared refund; consumed work/fanout
remain spent. Reads do not run, stop, retire or refund the producer. These checks
do not establish post-dispatch crash recovery or general rollback.
